Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

MultiTool Pro ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application and website.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data (such as your name, email address, phone number, or financial information) to our servers. All tools operate locally on your device.

2. Information We May Store Locally

To improve your experience, we store a few preferences locally on your device using localStorage:

  • Your chosen theme (light / dark).
  • Sound settings (on / off).

This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you clear your browser's cache.

We do not use any analytics, tracking cookies, or fingerprinting scripts.

Our tool is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

6. Your Rights
